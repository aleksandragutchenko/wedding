SELF-SERVICE HOMEWORK TEST

Try the complete finance workflow

This is a fictional, private test workspace. You can submit a transaction, act as its test manager, receive a Telegram decision, and reset only your own practice records. No access code or dashboard account is required.

Your workspace stays in this browser for 7 days. If you clear its cookies or use another browser, a new empty workspace starts. Your test records use the separate read-only Test Ledger sheet. The original Sales and Expenses tabs remain the submitted homework. This shared test sheet shows fictional transaction details only; it does not show your Telegram ID or private website session.

Starting your private test workspace…

1

Start the Telegram bot and get your Chat ID

Open @weddiing_hire_task_bot in Telegram. Press Start, then send /id. The bot replies with your numeric user ID and Chat ID. Use the Chat ID below. This must be your own private chat with the bot.

If Telegram does not open from this device, search for @weddiing_hire_task_bot in your Telegram app.

2

Verify and link your Telegram chat

Choose a fictional employee. The bot sends a confirmation button to the Chat ID you enter. Tap it in your private Telegram chat; this page will update automatically. The button works once and expires after 10 minutes. To test a second role with the same Telegram account, repeat this step after submitting the first transaction. Its original owner and notification chat stay saved.

3

Submit a test transaction

First verify a Telegram chat for the employee you will use. The destination is saved when you submit and cannot be added afterward. Use this form or send a full command to the bot from that linked chat. For a sale, send /sale ST101 | Sample customer | A | Sample wedding guests | 100.00 | 50/30/20. For Kevin’s expense, send /expense ET101 | Sample costume | Materials | 25.00 | A. References must be unique within your workspace. Refresh below to see bot submissions.

4

Make a test manager decision

The manager view below acts only on records from this browser’s test workspace. Approve a pending sale with a final commission split totaling 100%, or choose an expense allocation. You may change the proposed values. An expense proposed as Company overhead is allocated immediately, so choose project A or B to test an expense decision.

5

Check Telegram, website, and Google Sheets

The bot sends the decision to the chat saved on the transaction when it was created. Relinking the same Telegram account to another fictional employee later does not change that owner or destination. Refresh to compare the website status, delivery state, and totals. Open the Test Ledger sheet (Viewer access) and find your reference. The same row changes from Pending approval to Approved, while proposed and final splits remain visible. If a Sheet or Telegram delivery fails, retry it beside your record.

6

Reset your practice records

This removes only your workspace’s test transactions from the active view. It does not affect other testers or S01–S05/E01–E07. Reset cannot be undone from this page.
