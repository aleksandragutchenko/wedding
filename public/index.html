How the totals are calculated

Approved income includes only sales Svetlana has approved. Each approved sale creates a commission pool of 10% of that sale, divided using the final approved percentages. Commission is an expense of the sale’s project.

Every saved expense reduces the company result immediately. Expenses awaiting allocation do not yet reduce either project result. Company overhead is outside both projects.

Company result = approved income − commissions − all recorded expenses. Project result = that project’s approved income − its commissions − expenses allocated to it. Pending sales are excluded until approved.
